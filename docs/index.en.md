# Game Dev Companion

Extra material for the game development and XR modules at XJTLU. It covers the parts a
timetable cannot hold: the maths that sits underneath a shader, the vocabulary of VR, and
the small references that are faster to look up than to ask about.

!!! info "Every page exists in English and Chinese"
    Use the language selector at the top of the page. The two versions say the same thing.
    The Chinese one is written to be read, not translated word by word.

## Why this site exists

A 13-week module is roughly 60 hours of contact time. In that budget you can teach a
student to build a dissolve effect; there is no room left to explain why the normal map is
blue. Those explanations still matter, because without them the effect can be copied but
not changed, and the first time the requirement moves, the student is stuck again.

So the pages here take one question each and answer it properly. They are short on
purpose — most are a ten-minute read — and they always end with something you can check
against.

## How the site is organised

- **[Foundations](foundations/index.md)** — the maths and engine basics that all three
  modules assume. Start here if a formula looks like a wall.
- **[Shaders](shaders/index.md)** — what a shader actually computes, and why the effects
  you build in the lab behave the way they do.
- **[XR](xr/index.md)** — scale, comfort and the words VR uses.
- **[Glossary](glossary/index.md)** — English term, Chinese term, one line of meaning.
- **[Reference](reference/index.md)** — cheat sheets and setup notes, as they are written.
